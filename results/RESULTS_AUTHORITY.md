# Results authority

**Current export version:** `2026.11.10-eligible.1`

The authoritative results are the aggregate files in `analysis/` and
`results/eligible/`, generated by the production entry points in the repository
README. `results/eligible/RESULT_EXPORT_VERSION.json` records the export
version and artifact hashes.

The historical GitHub leaderboard (297 rows, with duplicate keys) and the Drive
leaderboard (275 rows) are preserved as historical snapshots only. Neither is a
substitute for the corrected eligible-analysis outputs. In particular:

- v6/v7 tissue results are trained-on-test diagnostics and are excluded from
  the primary eligible roster.
- Pairwise results from different evaluation manifests are not merged into one
  leaderboard.
- The five-class majority is computed from evaluated labels (43/163 = 0.264),
  not from the number of classes.
- Conditional MAE uses model-specific valid denominators; direct comparisons
  also report joint-valid sensitivity without discarding failure-aware metrics.

Legacy result files elsewhere in `results/` are historical unless explicitly
listed in `RELEASE_MANIFEST.json` as authoritative.
