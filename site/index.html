What this page shows

  • Tissue benchmark only. The MB-20xx liver tasks shown here. The cfRNA and blood-cohort families are part of the benchmark but are not shown here until their corrected exports are published.
  • Corrected numbers. Every cell comes from results/eligible/ in the repo at release 2026.11.10-eligible.1. Superseded leaderboards (297/275 rows) are retained only as historical snapshots.
  • Trained-on-test checkpoints excluded. Qwen3-0.6B v6, Qwen3-1.7B v6 and Qwen3-8B v7 saw the GSE135251 test split inside the fine-tuning mixtures; they are reported as diagnostics, never ranked.
  • Pairwise tasks are manifest-grouped. MB-2003 item sets differ by model family; results are not comparable across manifests and are never merged.

Headline read-out

  • Binary (MB-2001). No eligible model exceeds the 0.656 majority baseline; the leaders tie it.
  • Five-class (MB-2002). Best eligible result is 0.276 (Nemotron 3 Super 120B) vs a 0.264 majority baseline and 0.200 uniform-choice chance.
  • Fibrosis regression (MB-2005). The fine-tuned Qwen3-1.7B v5 leads on failure-aware MAE (1.142); Gemini 2.5 Pro produced no parseable answers on this task.
  • NAS regression (MB-2006). See the leaderboard; regression rows report conditional and failure-aware MAE and refresh-limited Spearman.

Figures on this page are generated from the release export; If they disagree with anything else online, the repo is authoritative.

Status

Research use only — not a diagnostic or clinical tool. Model access, particularly to fine-tuned checkpoints, is gated pending publication and author approval. See Methods for scope, corrections, and provenance, or the repository for code and exports.
