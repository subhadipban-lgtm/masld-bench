Task families

Every family is a frozen prompt corpus. Freeze discipline: SHA-256 manifests, test-before-train, and any correction gets a new freeze number with the old artifacts quarantined — corrections are published, not silently patched.

MB-20xx Liver transcriptomics — the frozen test

Bulk liver RNA-seq with biopsy endpoints: binary advanced fibrosis, F0–F4 staging (F-stage), pairwise comparisons, and regression (fibrosis stage / NAS). Gold = biopsy. Includes the EPos-only freeze after the documented Gubra label correction.

MB-85xx Progression proxy

Cross-sectional biopsy staging as a progression proxy (GSE185051, n=52). Binary stable-vs-progressive and ordinal score. Pre-registered as a proxy, not true paired-biopsy progression.

MB-86xx Blood-proxy severity

Whole-blood-cell RNA-seq with histology-graded severity (GSE267032, n=21 graded). Rebuilt at freeze-16 after the header off-by-one that mislabeled the freeze-8 corpus — the corrupted archives are quarantined and the correction is in the RUNLOG.

MB-87xx Liver→plasma proteomics

Masked-protein regression across plasma studies (MSV000094959, n=206–216) plus a platform readout. The platform task measures batch separation and is labeled as such.

MB-88xx Perturbation transfer

Primary hepatocyte FFA perturbation (GSE247407, n=30, donor-grouped) as a held-out transfer test for a 40-compound DRUG-seq train pool. The classical gate reads 0.833; LLMs sit at chance — zero-shot and trained-on. The program's controlled negative.

MB-89xx Plasma cell-free RNA — freeze-17 (newest family)

Plasma cfRNA transcriptomes (S-BSST1614, Ghent; n=112 biopsy-anchored + 179 external holdout). Label join verified 112/112 and 821/821 from the deposit itself. Three gold schemes frozen as separate task sets:

  • MB-8901/8902/8905 — biopsy Brunt F0–F4, reader A (UZA) gold
  • MB-8921/8922/8925 — identical prompt strings, reader B (Verheij/Doukas) gold: the dual-reader label-noise control (κ = 0.384)
  • MB-8911/8912/8915 — clinical-consensus F0–F4 gold, one sample per patient, per-item provenance flags, batch-clean (p = 0.332)

This is the family where the regime split inverts: frontier LLMs match the logistic regression on the binary task — the first LLM-positive omics-in-prompt result in the program.

Train-side pools

MB-10xx/11xx clinical and external liver pools, MB-12xx staged-liver pool (freeze-15), MB-40xx geometric manifold, MB-61xx/62xx/63xx knowledge corpora, MB-88xx DRUG-seq pool — all train-side only; the frozen tests above are never trained on.

Research use only. Task labels come from public deposits with verifiable joins; cohort 2 of MB-89xx uses clinical-consensus staging, disclosed per item.
