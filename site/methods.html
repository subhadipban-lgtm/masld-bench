Methods & provenance

What the benchmark measures, which data it uses, what was corrected, and what it does not claim.

What the benchmark is

MASLD-Bench probes whether large language models can interpret structured biological evidence for metabolic dysfunction–associated steatotic liver disease. Prompts encode frozen, whitespace-controlled evidence packets; models answer constrained tasks. Scoring is exact-match for classification/selection and numeric tolerance for regression, with paired bootstrap intervals.

The tissue arm (this page) covers the frozen MB-20xx test family built from the GSE135251 biopsy cohort, 163 common evaluated items for binary and five-class tasks, 162 for fibrosis regression, 163 for NAS regression.

Eligibility and exclusions

Primary roster. Models with all five tissue tasks, minus three checkpoints (Qwen3-0.6B v6, Qwen3-1.7B v6, Qwen3-8B v7) whose fine-tuning mixtures contained the MB-12xx staged-liver pool overlapping GSE135251. Those three appear only as exposed-data diagnostics and are never eligible for ranking.

Common items. Results are computed on archived common items only: 163 binary, 163 five-class, 162 fibrosis, 163 NAS. No re-imputation, no post-hoc swapping of labels.

Pairwise tasks. MB-2003 item sets differ across model families. Results are grouped by exact manifest (14 models × 300 shared items; DeepSeek and Nemotron on 258 in-corpus items; Gemini 2.5 Pro on 414 items) and are not merged into a single ranking.

Corrections that produced this export

  • The MB-86xx blood-proxy corpus was misaligned by a header off-by-one (freeze-8); rebuilt at freeze-16. Affected v5-zeroshot cells were retired.
  • The historical 297-row GitHub leaderboard and 275-row Drive leaderboard are not the current authority; they are historical snapshots.
  • The five-class majority is computed from evaluated labels (43/163 = 0.264), not from the number of classes.
  • cfRNA label-noise: AMC-vs-UZA reader disagreement is retained per item; the 0.384 kappa and the “13-point reader-noise ceiling” from earlier materials are withdrawn.
  • Claims of “first LLM-positive” and “statistically tied” were withdrawn; equivalence is not implied by non-significance.

Reading the failure-aware MAE

Regression answers can be missing or unparseable. MAE (all, raw) divides total absolute error by all items including failures. MAE in-domain conditions on parseable, in-domain answers. MAE failure-aware assigns unparseable outputs the task’s worst-case error instead of dropping them. When these diverge sharply, a model’s headline number partly reflects output formatting rather than biology.

Data access & licensing

The corpora are prompt packs derived from public cohorts (GSE135251 for the tissue test; cfRNA S-BSST1614 for the cfRNA arm). Source GEO/SRA data remain under their original terms; this repository distributes code and aggregate results only. See DATA_ACCESS_AND_LICENSES.md in the repo for the exact access path and redistribution limits. Nothing on this page is clinical evidence.

Reproduce

With the pinned environment and a MASLD_DATA_ROOT holding the frozen corpora:

.venv/bin/python analysis/rescore_tissue_eligible.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest analysis/tests -q
bash scripts/reproduce_release.sh

The release manifest RELEASE_MANIFEST.json records file hashes for the authoritative export.
