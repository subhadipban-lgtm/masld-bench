Methods

The benchmark's scientific contribution is as much how it measures as what it measures. Four disciplines, all pre-registered.

1 · Pre-registration and the frozen corpus

Every analysis plan is frozen before any evaluation call is made. Prompt corpora are checksummed (SHA-256 manifests) and never regenerated; tests are frozen before any training data is added. Corrections — the Gubra label fix, the freeze-16 blood-corpus off-by-one — get new freeze numbers and the corrupted artifacts are quarantined, never silently repaired. Column order is never evidence: every label join must be verifiable from the deposit itself.

2 · Paired significance (McNemar exact / Wilcoxon signed-rank)

Headline comparisons are made on identical items, not on separately sampled accuracies. The bracket's frontier draws were re-evaluated on the same 929-item frame so every C2 contrast is paired. The discipline already paid for itself: the program's own unpaired "win" evaporated once the bracket was completed and paired — reported, not hidden.

3 · Classical gates (the C4 regime split)

Every family carries pre-registered logistic-regression / histogram-gradient-boosting gates on the same prompt strings (grouped CV, 2,000 bootstraps, seed 20260929). The LLM-vs-classical comparison on identical inputs is what turns "LLMs fail" into a precise, testable statement about representation format — and what made the cfRNA inversion (C5) discoverable.

4 · Label-noise control (dual-reader golds)

Biopsy staging is reader-dependent. Instead of pretending otherwise, the MB-89xx anchor freezes both independent pathologists' readings as separate task sets with byte-identical prompts. The measured cap: 13 accuracy points for the classical reader, ~3 for frontier LLMs, at κ = 0.384. Any machine-learning study on biopsy labels inherits this ceiling; this benchmark is the first to measure it directly.

Decoding protocol (frozen)

  • Zero-shot, single pass, temperature 0.0, top_p 1.0
  • Invalid or unparseable answers count as errors (invalid-as-error)
  • Bootstrap 95% CIs, 10,000 resamples, seed 20260929
  • Frontier bracket: 12 systems via OpenRouter; compact models: greedy, newline-stopped
  • Training-exposure controls: masked-gene variants and trained-on-vs-held-out transfer tests
Research use only. These methods evaluate frozen retrospective corpora; they do not validate any clinical use.
