Reading this table honestly

  • Aggregate metrics only. No subject-level data, prompts, or predictions are distributed on this page.
  • Exposed checkpoints are not eligible. Qwen3-0.6B v6, Qwen3-1.7B v6 and Qwen3-8B v7 saw the MB-12xx training pool overlapping the test split and are excluded from ranking; their cells appear in the release as diagnostics only.
  • MB-2003 is not a single leaderboard. Pairwise results are reported per item manifest; do not compare across manifests.
  • Non-significance is not equivalence. Absence of a detectable difference is not evidence of no difference.
