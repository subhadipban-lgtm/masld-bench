Why the cfRNA signal is readable

Not cell-type markers (0/20 of the top informative genes overlap the deposit's hepatocyte/LSEC Gini table). The signal concentrates in ~5 genes — P4HA2, ELN, AMOTL2, RGS5, SREBF1 — fibrosis-relevant biology. A label-informed top-5 panel reaches 71.4% out-of-fold accuracy vs 72.3% for the full 200-gene panel. A few strongly deviating values are readable by any model class; diffuse panel-wide signal is not. That concentration is the mechanism behind the conditional regime split.

Research use only. These fine-tunes are research artifacts trained on public corpora. They are not validated for any clinical, diagnostic, or patient-facing use, and their outputs on this benchmark are frozen-corpus evaluations, not real-world performance.
